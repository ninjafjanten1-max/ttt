How it works: A face-landmark model finds 68 points on your face. The photo is rotated so your eyes are level, then the app measures (1) left/right symmetry around the facial midline and (2) how close key proportions (eye spacing, nose width, mouth width, facial thirds) are to classical "ideal" ratios such as 1 : 1 and the golden ratio 1.618.

Important: this is a fun math toy, not a measure of your worth or real-world attractiveness. Attractiveness depends on expression, skin, style, culture, personality and much more. Your photo never leaves your device.
