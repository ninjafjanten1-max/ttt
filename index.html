How it works: A 478-point face-mesh model (MediaPipe) locates your eye corners, irises, nose, mouth and jaw in 3D. The app first frontalizes the face — it removes head roll and left/right turn using the 3D landmarks, so a slightly turned head no longer squashes the eye ratios. It then measures symmetry around the facial midline and compares key proportions (eye spacing, eye width vs. face width, canthal tilt, nose, mouth, facial thirds) to classical ideals such as "one eye-width apart" and the golden ratio.

Important: this is a fun math toy, not a measure of your worth or real-world attractiveness. Attractiveness depends on expression, skin, style, culture, personality and much more. Your photo never leaves your device.
